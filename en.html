FAMILY SOCIAL PLAN · 2027—2031
Food support

“Durandueña” Food Basket

Bringing the essentials home.

10,000households as the goal

The “Durandueña” Food Basket is the Family Social Plan's food assistance proposal. It targets 10,000 vulnerable households in the canton and proposes monthly food baskets during Rodrigo Aparicio's proposed municipal administration.

Food is an everyday household need. This initiative therefore proposes regular support within a plan that also brings together health, education and entrepreneurship opportunities. Its proposed coverage aims to reach the families who need this recurring assistance most.

What it includes

  • Food baskets distributed monthly.
  • Food assistance for vulnerable households.
  • A coverage goal of 10,000 households in the canton.
  • Continuity throughout the proposed municipal administration.

Who is it for?

Vulnerable households in Durán, through the plan's household registration and eligibility process.

Program implementation

The program would join the municipality's unified registration and follow-up system. Its institutional implementation would define funding, coverage, public eligibility requirements and evaluation procedures.

FAMILY SOCIAL PLAN · 2027—2031
Entrepreneurship

Entrepreneurship School

Tools for taking the next step.

2,500people each year

The Entrepreneurship School proposes free training for 2,500 people each year. It brings together management tools, digital skills and practical trades to support people seeking to develop a productive activity.

The program covers administration, sales, digital marketing, artificial intelligence, costs and business formalization. It also includes support for productive activities. Training and assistance with developing a business are proposed as parts of the same initiative.

Training areas

  • Administration and costs for business management.
  • Sales and digital marketing.
  • Artificial intelligence applied to learning and productive activities.
  • Practical trades and business formalization.
  • Support for productive activities.

Who is it for?

People seeking training to start or strengthen a productive activity in Durán. The proposed annual goal is 2,500 participants.

Program implementation

Training is proposed as a free institutional program. Coverage, public eligibility requirements and evaluation would be defined as part of its implementation within the social plan.

Animal welfare

Municipal Veterinary Clinic

Pets are part of the family.

Caring for themmeans caring for the family

The proposed Municipal Veterinary Clinic includes pets among the family card's benefits. The plan covers basic consultations, spaying and neutering, vaccination and parasite treatment as part of veterinary care.

Its scope also includes rescued animals and responsible ownership campaigns. The program brings together care for participating families' pets, preventive services and support for rescued animals. Animal care and responsible ownership are part of this commitment.

Planned services

  • Basic veterinary consultations.
  • Spaying and neutering.
  • Vaccination and parasite treatment.
  • Care for rescued animals.
  • Responsible pet ownership campaigns.

Who is it for?

Pets belonging to participating families through their family card, as well as the rescued animals covered by the proposal.

Program implementation

The clinic would be one of the plan's seven initial programs, with funding, coverage and public eligibility requirements defined during institutional implementation.

Community health

Municipal and Community Health Center

Caring for health every day.

Preventionand ongoing health support

The Municipal and Community Health Center is proposed as a primary care facility for people enrolled in the social plan. Its scope includes prevention, chronic disease management and medical guidance.

The proposal states that these services would be developed through an agreement with health authorities. This coordination is part of the program: the center would provide a coordinated approach to family care, consultations and health support within primary care.

Areas of care

  • Primary care for enrolled participants.
  • Prevention and health care.
  • Chronic disease management.
  • Medical guidance.
  • Coordination through an agreement with health authorities.

Who is it for?

People enrolled in the Family Social Plan, within the coverage established for the center.

Program implementation

Implementation depends on the proposed health agreement and on defining funding, public eligibility requirements and evaluation procedures.

Family support

Funeral Assistance

Support through difficult times too.

By your sidewhen it matters most

When a member of a participating household dies, the plan proposes assistance through authorized fee exemptions or subsidies for municipal cemetery services.

This initiative is part of the plan's support for families at important moments. It applies specifically to municipal cemetery services and authorized exemption or subsidy mechanisms. Assistance at these times is one of the Family Social Plan's seven initial programs.

Proposed assistance

  • Support after the loss of a participating household member.
  • Authorized fee exemptions or subsidies.
  • Coverage of municipal cemetery services.
  • Inclusion in the plan's unified registration and follow-up system.

Who is it for?

Enrolled households experiencing the loss of one of their members, subject to the conditions established for the program.

Program implementation

Funding, coverage and eligibility requirements would be made public as part of the program's institutional implementation.

FAMILY SOCIAL PLAN · 2027—2031
Education

University Outreach and Scholarships

Opening more doors through education.

Learningto open new paths

The plan proposes agreements with universities and institutes to bring free academic programs, continuing education, technical training and community outreach to Durán. The proposal builds on discussions initiated with these institutions.

The proposed areas reflect courses requested by young people during visits around the city. They span technology, engineering, renewable energy, services and creative activities. University outreach and scholarships depend on the agreements reached; the listed areas represent the proposed academic scope.

Types of education

  • Academic programs through university and institute agreements.
  • Continuing education.
  • Technical training.
  • Community outreach.
  • University outreach and student scholarships.

Proposed study areas

Technology and data

Cybersecurity · Artificial Intelligence and Data Science · Software Development and Cloud · Computer Engineering and AI · Software, Systems and Cybersecurity Engineering.

Engineering and energy

Renewable Energy and Photovoltaic Systems · Industrial Automation and Mechatronics · Electrical Engineering and Renewable Energy · Mechatronics and Automation Engineering · Mining Engineering and Geology.

Services and creativity

Culinary Arts · Marketing · Tourism · International Trade · Graphic Design and other areas.

Who is it for?

Young people in Durán and others interested in the educational opportunities covered by the agreements.

Program implementation

Courses, coverage and eligibility requirements would be coordinated with participating universities and institutes as part of the program's institutional implementation.

Household opportunities

Household Seed Funding

A boost for household projects.

Supportingyour family's next step

Household Seed Funding proposes financial assistance for eligible enrolled households. Support is intended for three purposes: higher education, business development and improvements to housing infrastructure.

This support recognizes that family projects can follow different paths: continuing education, developing a productive activity or improving living conditions. Access to assistance would depend on household eligibility and the purposes established in the proposal.

Three purposes for assistance

  • Higher education.
  • Business development.
  • Housing infrastructure improvements.

Who is it for?

Enrolled households meeting the eligibility requirements for financial assistance.

Program implementation

The program would have public eligibility requirements, funding, coverage and evaluation procedures.
